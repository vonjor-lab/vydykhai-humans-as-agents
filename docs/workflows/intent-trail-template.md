# Intent Trail Template

Use one compact shared current view per project or stream when meaningful human intent or the first significant approach pivot must survive beyond one context. Idea Memory holds future options; Intent Trail holds active intent, working rules, and why important decisions changed.

It is not a transcript, backlog, or copy of every instruction. Message length is irrelevant. Preserve the smallest useful lineage and link the source.

```md
# Intent Trail: <project or stream>

Purpose: preserve current human intent and the reasoning path needed to avoid repeating rejected approaches.
Project State: <link>
Last reconciliation: <date/checkpoint>

| ID | Type / status | Now | Before / why | Keep / drop | Applies to | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| INT-01 | <INTENT | WORKING_RULE | APPROACH_PIVOT> / <CONFIRMED | PROVISIONAL | SUPERSEDED> | <current intent or approach> | <previous approach and evidence for change> | <proven parts to preserve / path not to repeat> | <task, surfaces, contracts, or class of work> | <message, meeting, task, or evidence link> |
```

Rules:

- Capture semantic impact, not verbosity. Explicit remember/important/always/never/do-it-differently language and a human-approved pivot are `CONFIRMED`; inferred wider applicability is `PROVISIONAL` and must be echoed once for correction.
- `APPROACH_PIVOT` applies even when DOD is unchanged and the change is operational or task-local, if it replaces the main method, layer, baseline, sequence, boundary, or verification route.
- Keep task-local lineage in the task issue or context and include it in Return Sync. Promote it here only when it affects another task, a reusable mechanism, a project quality bar, or future planning.
- Do not copy full messages by default. Preserve a privacy-safe summary and source link; quote or copy exact text only when the human requests it or the source is not durable and the wording is necessary.
- At planning, resume, acceptance, and rotation, reconcile applicable entries with current decisions and task evidence. A new orchestrator must reconstruct the current approach and why material pivots replaced earlier ones.
- After acceptance, compact the active view to the current rule, decisive reasons, Keep/Drop lessons, and source links. Mark superseded entries and retain their evidence trail instead of deleting the path.
