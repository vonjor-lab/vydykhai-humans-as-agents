# Independent Control On The Supported Route

Opt-in controlled operating contract, shipped in managed workflows, not proof of activation or product-change authority.
Read at adoption, new admission, product acceptance, an independent finding or due Health Review, not every unchanged turn.

## Select And Prove The Mode

When adopting this operating contract, the setup owner must establish the actual
supported mode and retain it in the existing activation record. Do not leave its
selection or first observation for a later user reminder. Use the private runtime
activation/startup route in the canonical source's `BOOTSTRAP.md` (not an installed
product file), with trusted source identity, actual
permissions, a finite model budget and an owned return path. Preserve in-flight
results, accepted work, explicit pauses and spent allocations at a safe boundary.

Controlled means an actual selected protected host with independent REVIEW and
registered command admission. A copied kit, process name, different actor strings,
prompt instruction or self-issued receipt is not that proof. Native tools outside
this supported route are not intercepted. Unsupported authority, scheduling or
delivery is LIMITED with an owner and next review; never claim controlled adoption
or silently switch dependent work to a legacy execution path. Independent work may
retain its already accepted mode. No activation automatically resumes paused work.

The host is a transport/admission service, not another product manager. The existing
orchestrator owns outcome, scope, ordering and knowledge integration; the existing
executor owns implementation and technical proof; existing REVIEW supplies fresh
independent judgments. Preparation gathers facts under the task owner. No new role
is needed and no participant certifies another participant's environment.

Optimize the total effort to achieve and retain accepted outcomes, not the cost of
control in isolation. Preparation and review may cost more when they prevent
observed downstream rework, forgotten decisions, duplicate modules and excess
context. Measure those losses across subsequent tasks alongside all operating
costs; neither a cheaper first task nor a green checklist proves improvement.
Never remove required evidence just to make control cheaper, or claim savings from
hypothetical failures that did not occur in a comparable observed sequence.

## Before A New Action

Use the accepted module, map and graph routes to prepare the actual task. Recover
applicable decisions, rejected approaches, success conditions and due ideas without
opening consumed modules. `context-preparation.md` owns the package format; owner
confirmation is not an independent review or a worker's comprehension receipt.

The event-bound route requires host `requirePreparation:true` and `acceptPreparation(packageAcceptance,{binding,reviewSourceIds})`.
It atomically accepts the prepared package and enrolls its independent review, not future output. Include original sources and their authoritative inventory, not only the preparer's selection.
Readiness precedes execution: judge current inputs, decisions, rejected approaches and permissions; never require future output as permission to produce it.
`binding` is one exact command binding or an explicit array for action and verification, including operation, executable, confinement and all digests. Different bindings cannot borrow readiness.
The trusted host may preregister a dormant exact `executionRequest`, with finite expiry (at most one hour on this strict route); registration is not execution approval.
Current independent verdict and owner application are mandatory at authorization and reservation. The existing continuation handler consumes only that exact registered request.
The executor reads and acknowledges actual delivery. A free-form receipt, executor identity, missing/rejected/stale review or changed binding cannot grant admission.
Legacy policy remains historical evidence, not proof of mandatory PRE. Package approval never certifies task output; technical verification, owning acceptance and POST remain separate.

Technical verification still occurs before owning acceptance. It does not replace
independent post-accept control. A successful command, `VERIFIED` receipt or request
to run a command is not proof of a completed product outcome or observed effect.
Do not manufacture a missing readback to keep a workflow within a model-call cap.

## After Owning Acceptance

The orchestrator first consumes the exact producer Return, judges the promised
result and records actual memory, map, tracker and custody changes. Retain failed
writes as pending obligations. Do not substitute a promise to update later, copy a
producer Return into a new author identity, or accept a whole parent from a slice.

For the controlled route, record that owning decision through the selected host's
`accept` API, with exact product/Return/technical-proof/goal references and retention
readback. Acceptance durably enrolls fresh post-accept REVIEW. This enrollment is
mandatory even when the orchestrator considers its work complete; the executor's
technical review and owner-authored package records cannot satisfy it.

The trusted host binds `reviewSourceIds` per immutable acceptance instead of an unrelated registry.
Discovery stays broad; bound sources must be acquired, but selection does not prove completeness.
Before the first review is claimed, the owner may use additive `recordSourceScopeExpansion` to bind newly discovered evidence without changing the acceptance or inventing a failed attempt. The host must be unpaused, with no review, lease, episode, attempt or repair history, and no dependent action in flight. The original scope and every expansion remain recorded. Once a review has started, preserve stopped output, reconcile, then expand before fresh review; never drop evidence or replace original scope. Both paths require the current scope hash and available registered sources; neither grants result use. Use the current runtime's preserving switch for upgrade or rollback. After a preinitial expansion it requires the target's validated `reviewSourceScope` readback; retaining raw journal bytes is not compatibility. An older reader without this capability is refused. Keep the current reader paused and preserve history instead of erasing revisions to permit a downgrade.
Exact markers bind shared memory/map meaning; missing, duplicate or changed meaning/markers invalidate proof.

The selected service observes the due episode under its existing finite allocation.
REVIEW starts without the manager's explanatory dialogue, obtains original/current
sources independently, and considers all 28 catalog groups. Each applicable
criterion needs current evidence; NOT_APPLICABLE needs a scoped reason. Reuse valid
evidence instead of repeating a whole-project audit. Missing evidence is not PASS.
The runtime supplies its versioned catalog; the worker cannot replace it with a shorter checklist. Host-bound reviews receive the complete current source index inline, independently of the preparer. This removes a mandatory discovery round trip, not original-source acquisition. The index is untrusted metadata, not read evidence, a whitelist or completeness proof; `list_sources` remains available and scoped-source expansion still requires its existing authorization.
Whole-source `read_source` accepts one `id` or a selected batch of `ids` (up to32, reply up to256 KiB). Batching changes transport only: every original remains independently hashed, acquired and checked; the receipt and resumed assignment retain those exact reads. Oversized batches fail without hidden partial acquisition or truncation. Durable native reviewers use direct source/submission tools, avoiding the intermediate code-mode output cap; the history-output allowance covers the existing reply bound, not larger source permissions. Compatibility must prove full bytes in the next model input, not merely the MCP log. Fresh reviews do not inherit another review's reads or judgments. Compact PASS rows may omit redundant optional explanations, never required evidence, catalog coverage, applicability reasons or meaningful negative/result-use decisions. Repeated oversized acquisition uses optional trusted [original-source windows](review-acquisition.md), not a shorter catalog or another role; windowed profiles retain per-window accounting. Preserve all originals, complete required acquisition, source-bound gaps and cumulative expenditure. A text allowance is not total token/cost proof; local protocol checks do not establish native return or autonomous adoption.
Fixed parts recheck the host lease and source basis before dispatch and complete-union admission. Opt-in host-bound POST and HEALTH episodes check one frozen whole-group part per tick, persist its validated native observation before progress, and retain it across reopen. Only the complete 28-group/87-criterion union exposes an owner verdict; a checkpoint is not repair scope or permission and grants no acceptance or execution authority. The caller owns the frozen plan, finite allocation, duration and usage envelope. Continue successful parts without replay; known failure needs independent changed-attempt reconciliation, unknown termination or usage holds. Spend and expiry never reset; source, execution, plan drift or pause invalidates reuse even after restoration. Completed-invalid candidates retain trace/usage. HEALTH episodes support diagnosis and repair verification; captured confined-command domains remain single-review and refuse episodes. Existing single/fixed-part profiles are unchanged. Select a justified small number of parts, not a call per criterion. Deferred observation is not completed diagnosis or project readiness. New allocation requires explicit authority and cumulative accounting, not a larger timeout, exhausted-profile rewrite or another timer. A source read is not a semantic verdict.
The orchestrator must read the exact verdict and apply an observable disposition:
assign bounded repair with owner/checkpoint for blocking findings, or continue the
permitted next step while retaining nonblocking obligations. Acknowledgment or a
chat reply without application leaves control pending. A manager's explanation
cannot erase the original finding, lower its criterion, or reset failed attempts.
Read-only investigation may resolve disagreement; closure needs fresh evidence.

Keep the accepted product artifact while repairing control or retention. Fresh REVIEW preserves original findings and failure history. Default profiles cover the full catalog; reuse unaffected evidence only while current and applicable. Partial coverage is not full coverage.

Opt-in host `reviewPolicy:PHASED_V1` requires HEALTH, registered evidence and mandatory preparation. The existing catalog owns the fixed lifecycle allocation: full87 HEALTH,45 PRE and59 POST criteria, extended by every retained finding. PRE owns prior purpose/decisions/rejections and intended action; POST owns actual application, accepted result, retained knowledge and cleanup. Each task gate still checks authority, exact inputs, independent result-use and all required writes. Rules assigned elsewhere are not synthesized PASS or NOT_APPLICABLE; full global health is never inferred from a task verdict. Source access and fresh reviewer contexts remain unchanged. Relevant omitted obligations or unknown dependencies require C24.02 and UNKNOWN/REJECT, not an exclusion; ALLOW with unresolved C24.02 is mechanically refused. Policy and stage are bound into the review key; changed sources still invalidate acceptance. HEALTH triggers and blocking findings remain independent. This mode does not combine with partial-review episodes and does not silently migrate existing profiles.
Dependent actions wait; unrelated authorized work need not wait. A deadline grants no further approval, repair budget or authority.
Trusted-host POST `requestRepairReview(id, evidence, findingIds)` names only OPEN findings submitted as repaired; missing IDs are refused. Old stored evidence without this binding is never backfilled as a checked attempt.
A completed review counts each submitted finding left OPEN once as FAILED. Ordinary follow-up and stopped/no-verdict review invent no attempts; existing fresh-proof closure on source/retention follow-up remains supported.
After failure, separate `authorizeRepairRetry(id, {reviewer, findingId, fromAttempt, reviewKey, evidence, decision})` retains an independent changed-attempt decision against the current review and runnable repair lease.
HEALTH uses the same decision fields without the acceptance ID, including nonblocking repair in SETTLED. A decision cannot be reused for a later failure.

## Close Through Observed Disposition

CONTINUE needs a current package, real worker readback and observed authorized effect; plans, dispatch and acknowledgment are not completion.
If work is finished or legitimately waiting, do not invent a command: trusted SDK enrolls the durable `control.terminal-disposition.v1` artifact.
DONE binds accepted scope/DOD and producer Return; WAIT binds owner, trigger, return condition and review checkpoint. Both preserve acceptance.
Fresh REVIEW covering every assigned criterion reads the record, Return, goal and technical proof, then explicitly judges `terminalDisposition` at C20.03.
The owner acquires `read_verdict`, then applies WAIT/DONE with its exact record hash; closure/application are historical readback facts, not continuing readiness.
Neither grants execution nor clears blockers, pending review, pause or UNKNOWN. Selected current readback returns drift/expired WAIT to review; no external-writer atomic fence is claimed.

UNKNOWN effects retain the consumed grant and ownership; no automatic repeat, replacement owner or budget reset. Command timeout/output-limit teardown gets250ms grace; unobserved close returns UNKNOWN, not stopped-process proof. Local pipe destruction cannot clear a hold.
For captured command failures, trusted-host `reconcileCommand` requires independent exact termination and separate effect reconciliation, bound to the reservation and journal revision. `FAILED_RECONCILED` preserves failure, consumes the old grant and grants no success, retry or unpause; correction needs a current packet and fresh independent admission. `openControlService` accepts the observer only through its trusted SDK argument, never profile/JSON/MCP. Migration/cutover require the exact retained reconciliation and append-only transition, preserving pause and failed/spent history; unknown or forged settlement still blocks. Generic effect collection and scheduled CLI recovery remain LIMITED.
A missed result reaches the existing owner once; failed application stays visible as pending attention.
A durable notice is not a delivered human alert. If the supported return route
cannot deliver, report that precise limitation instead of declaring closure.

## Between Tasks And On Adoption

Use existing `startScheduledControl`: default profiles start on the first natural interval. Opt-in `eventDriven:true` requests a non-terminating wake of that same registered service after registration. Host `observationEvents:{descriptorFile,mode,intervalSeconds}` does the same after durable preparation/acceptance enrollment. A wake is neither work completion nor permission. No `-k`, worker interruption, retries or additional manager; the existing timer reconciles a lost/coalesced signal. Explicit pause blocks wake and admission. Registration is not completed startup.
`waitControlObservation` observes terminal receipts using `afterReceiptSha256`; `PENDING/WAIT_TIMEOUT` never kills, restarts or messages a worker. Intermediate updates under the same invocation are not completion.
Actual execution deadlines remain separate: an explicitly allocated review may use up to 900000ms, with service budget covering native work plus 1000ms; defaults and non-review worker caps stay unchanged. Receipt waiting remains capped at 300000ms. `waitControlEvent` can passively wait for a named control application's outcome with an explicit observation duration up to one day; the transport must allow that wait. It does not run a tick or cancel work. Intermediate unchanged status stays in code, not repeated model turns. Neither a longer work deadline nor paused legacy startup authorizes another attempt or proves completion.

The existing owner delivery handler also supports explicit `EXACT_CONTROL_DISPOSITION` delegation: independently allowed HEALTH continuation, a named preparation-to-task mapping, or independently bound WAIT/DONE. It uses the same current-verdict, source, pause and application-readback checks, with zero model calls. Open findings, negative/unknown judgments and unmapped decisions remain with the existing owner; there is no automatic model fallback. Semantic owner acceptance still happens before POST, and independent PRE/POST still use fresh reviewers. Unknown application remains held, never replayed. Native delegation is unchanged; selecting exact delegation is explicit, not a silent profile migration.
For zero-model owner waiting, configure `ownerDelivery.waits`: save the checkpoint, end the native turn and resume that exact session only after a mechanically verified event. With `waits.eventDriven:true`, native exit checks for an already available result; a trusted role-completion adapter calls the same `waiting.observe()` after persisting its Return. Neither callback dispatches a model or chooses the next task. The periodic observer remains recovery, and OBSERVATION waits remain timer-owned for a genuine quiet interval. A long pending MCP call is not equivalent. Pause/unknown exit block resumption; events grant no task permission. See [durable waiting](../design/local-control-host.md#durable-owner-waiting) for transport and accounting boundaries.
For ordinary zero-model reconciliation select `health.periodicReview:false`: elapsed time alone spends no call; source triggers, pending/missing review registration, leases and overdue findings remain observed. Initial HEALTH and explicit reset-cadence obligations are preserved. Unchanged sweeps are read-only; unregistered external work remains outside coverage. Existing owners handle actual exceptions.

Initial protected-host diagnosis requires independent review and the owner's actual
application. Afterwards a due or in-flight periodic review remains an owned obligation,
not a global execution hold. Blocking HEALTH findings may carry explicit source-backed
`affectedTasks`; absent scope is global/unknown. The scope survives unchanged in finding
history. Host-wide safety or authority failure must not be narrowed to unrelated tasks.
Only affected work waits; an actually owned repair lease can admit its exact repair
task without a static alias. Explicit pauses and failed-repair retry gates remain.
Known repair work cannot fall through as unrelated execution: every finding served
by that task needs a runnable lease and any required independent retry decision.
WAITING establishes ownership, not readiness. A fresh closure lifts an existing
affected hold only after owner application; unrelated work continues meanwhile.

Registry-backed services bind REVIEW to their own protected host. POST/HEALTH checks every frozen part and requires the complete union before a host verdict; a part alone grants nothing.
Legacy single-part `bindHost` requires a trusted owner, paused host, stopped model runs and no in-flight command; it preserves calls/runs/expiry without resetting budget or unpausing.
Legacy multipart rebinding is unsupported: retain history and diagnose an owned compatible transition, never downgrade or guess an unauthenticated configuration.

Initial adoption includes owned recovery or evidenced NO_CHANGE for existing maps, module boundaries, graph/idea retention, tracker and artifact custody.
Missing full mapping is not completed mapping; a modularization proposal is not a sealed module.
Reuse existing Maintenance and preserve the remaining whole-project obligations.
Measure independently closed debt and retained product progress together; deferral,
renaming and deletion of a record are not repair. Follow `framework-activation.md`
for safe ordering and human decisions, without a compulsory whole-project rewrite.

Report product acceptance, control disposition, adoption limits, remaining owners, actual continuation and total observed effort separately. Neither a green checklist
nor this document proves universal reliability or lower subscription consumption.
