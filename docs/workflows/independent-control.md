# Independent Control On The Supported Route

Opt-in controlled operating contract. This procedure is shipped through the
managed workflows directory; it is not proof of an activated host or permission
to change a product. Read it at controlled-mode adoption, new admission, product
acceptance, an independent finding, or a due Health Review, not every unchanged turn.

## Select And Prove The Mode

When adopting this operating contract, the setup owner must establish the actual
supported mode and retain it in the existing activation record. Do not leave its
selection or first observation for a later user reminder. Use the private runtime
activation/startup route in the canonical source's `BOOTSTRAP.md` (not an installed
product file), with trusted source identity, actual
permissions, a finite model budget and an owned return path. Preserve in-flight
results, accepted work, explicit pauses and spent allocations at a safe boundary.

Controlled means an actual selected protected host with independent REVIEW and
registered command admission. A copied kit, process name, different actor strings,
prompt instruction or self-issued receipt is not that proof. Native tools outside
this supported route are not intercepted. Unsupported authority, scheduling or
delivery is LIMITED with an owner and next review; never claim controlled adoption
or silently switch dependent work to a legacy execution path. Independent work may
retain its already accepted mode. No activation automatically resumes paused work.

The host is a transport/admission service, not another product manager. The existing
orchestrator owns outcome, scope, ordering and knowledge integration; the existing
executor owns implementation and technical proof; existing REVIEW supplies fresh
independent judgments. Preparation gathers facts under the task owner. No new role
is needed and no participant certifies another participant's environment.

Optimize the total effort to achieve and retain accepted outcomes, not the cost of
control in isolation. Preparation and review may cost more when they prevent
observed downstream rework, forgotten decisions, duplicate modules and excess
context. Measure those losses across subsequent tasks alongside all operating
costs; neither a cheaper first task nor a green checklist proves improvement.
Never remove required evidence just to make control cheaper, or claim savings from
hypothetical failures that did not occur in a comparable observed sequence.

## Before A New Action

Use the accepted module, map and graph routes to prepare the actual task. Recover
applicable decisions, rejected approaches, success conditions and due ideas without
opening consumed modules. `context-preparation.md` owns the package format; owner
confirmation is not an independent review or a worker's comprehension receipt.

Before affected mutation, a fresh independent readiness check must consider the
applicable obligations and original sources, not only the preparer's selection.
Readiness is pre-execution: inspect required authorized inputs/specifications, current public wiring and retained baseline. Require the new action's actual output at verification/acceptance/POST, never as permission to produce that output.
Bind its disposition to the current package, permitted command and source revisions; required public specifications/manifests must be readable to the reviewer, not merely named or hashed.
The trusted host may then register the exact grant. The executor reads and
acknowledges the real current delivery and uses the registered controlled boundary.
Names or evidence supplied by the executor cannot grant itself admission. A changed
binding invalidates affected proof; an unchanged authorized continuation reuses it.

Technical verification still occurs before owning acceptance. It does not replace
independent post-accept control. A successful command, `VERIFIED` receipt or request
to run a command is not proof of a completed product outcome or observed effect.
Do not manufacture a missing readback to keep a workflow within a model-call cap.

## After Owning Acceptance

The orchestrator first consumes the exact producer Return, judges the promised
result and records actual memory, map, tracker and custody changes. Retain failed
writes as pending obligations. Do not substitute a promise to update later, copy a
producer Return into a new author identity, or accept a whole parent from a slice.

For the controlled route, record that owning decision through the selected host's
`accept` API, with exact product/Return/technical-proof/goal references and retention
readback. Acceptance durably enrolls fresh post-accept REVIEW. This enrollment is
mandatory even when the orchestrator considers its work complete; the executor's
technical review and owner-authored package records cannot satisfy it.

The trusted host binds `reviewSourceIds` per immutable acceptance instead of an unrelated registry.
Discovery stays broad; bound sources must be acquired, but selection does not prove completeness.
For discovered dependencies, preserve stopped output, reconcile, then use owned additive `recordSourceScopeExpansion` before fresh review; never drop evidence or replace original scope.
Exact markers bind shared memory/map meaning; missing, duplicate or changed meaning/markers invalidate proof.

The selected service observes the due episode under its existing finite allocation.
REVIEW starts without the manager's explanatory dialogue, obtains original/current
sources independently, and considers all 28 catalog groups. Each applicable
criterion needs current evidence; NOT_APPLICABLE needs a scoped reason. Reuse valid
evidence instead of repeating a whole-project audit. Missing evidence is not PASS.
The runtime supplies its versioned catalog; the worker cannot replace it with a
shorter checklist. The verdict still covers the full catalog; partitioning is not repair scope or permission. Fixed parts recheck the current host lease and source basis before dispatch and complete-union admission. Opt-in host-bound POST episodes check one frozen whole-group part per tick; persist actual completed observations before progress and retain them across reopen. No partial owner verdict or permission. Known failure needs independent reconciliation; UNKNOWN holds, spend/expiry never reset, and observed relevant drift or pause invalidates reuse even after restoration. Full catalog/history validation precedes reusable verdict exposure; completed-invalid candidates retain trace/usage and need explicit independent retry, never an automatic new call. HEALTH retains its existing fixed-part route; REVIEW grants no repair or execution authority.

The orchestrator must read the exact verdict and apply an observable disposition:
assign bounded repair with owner/checkpoint for blocking findings, or continue the
permitted next step while retaining nonblocking obligations. Acknowledgment or a
chat reply without application leaves control pending. A manager's explanation
cannot erase the original finding, lower its criterion, or reset failed attempts.
Read-only investigation may resolve disagreement; closure needs fresh evidence.

Keep the accepted product artifact while repairing control or retention. Fresh REVIEW preserves original findings and failure history. The verdict still covers the full catalog; reuse unaffected evidence only while current and applicable. Partial coverage is not full coverage.
Dependent actions wait; unrelated authorized work need not wait. A deadline grants no further approval, repair budget or authority.
Trusted-host POST `requestRepairReview(id, evidence, findingIds)` names only OPEN findings submitted as repaired; missing IDs are refused. Old stored evidence without this binding is never backfilled as a checked attempt.
A completed review counts each submitted finding left OPEN once as FAILED. Ordinary follow-up and stopped/no-verdict review invent no attempts; existing fresh-proof closure on source/retention follow-up remains supported.
After failure, separate `authorizeRepairRetry(id, {reviewer, findingId, fromAttempt, reviewKey, evidence, decision})` retains an independent changed-attempt decision against the current review and runnable repair lease.
HEALTH uses the same decision fields without the acceptance ID, including nonblocking repair in SETTLED. A decision cannot be reused for a later failure.

## Close Through Observed Disposition

CONTINUE needs a current package, real worker readback and observed authorized effect; plans, dispatch and acknowledgment are not completion.
If work is finished or legitimately waiting, do not invent a command: trusted SDK enrolls the durable `control.terminal-disposition.v1` artifact.
DONE binds accepted scope/DOD and producer Return; WAIT binds owner, trigger, return condition and review checkpoint. Both preserve acceptance.
Fresh complete-catalog REVIEW reads the record, Return, goal and technical proof, then explicitly judges `terminalDisposition` at C20.03.
The owner acquires `read_verdict`, then applies WAIT/DONE with its exact record hash; closure/application are historical readback facts, not continuing readiness.
Neither grants execution nor clears blockers, pending review, pause or UNKNOWN. Selected current readback returns drift/expired WAIT to review; no external-writer atomic fence is claimed.

UNKNOWN effects retain the consumed grant and ownership; no automatic repeat, replacement owner or budget reset. Command timeout/output-limit teardown gets250ms grace; unobserved close returns UNKNOWN, not stopped-process proof. Local pipe destruction cannot clear a hold.
For captured command failures, trusted-host `reconcileCommand` requires independent exact termination and separate effect reconciliation, bound to the reservation and journal revision. `FAILED_RECONCILED` preserves failure, consumes the old grant and grants no success, retry or unpause; correction needs a current packet and fresh independent admission. `openControlService` accepts the observer only through its trusted SDK argument, never profile/JSON/MCP. Migration/cutover require the exact retained reconciliation and append-only transition, preserving pause and failed/spent history; unknown or forged settlement still blocks. Generic effect collection and scheduled CLI recovery remain LIMITED.
A missed result reaches the existing owner once; failed application stays visible as pending attention.
A durable notice is not a delivered human alert. If the supported return route
cannot deliver, report that precise limitation instead of declaring closure.

## Between Tasks And On Adoption

Enroll independent project and orchestration Health Review due conditions outside
the manager's conversation. Use the agreed reset-aware window when reset metadata
is supported, otherwise the explicitly accepted interval with its limits. Cheap
observation does not launch models for unchanged state. Relevant new facts, a due
checkpoint or repeated no-progress trigger a bounded fresh review under its budget.

Initial protected-host diagnosis requires independent review and the owner's actual
application. Afterwards a due or in-flight periodic review remains an owned obligation,
not a global execution hold. Blocking HEALTH findings may carry explicit source-backed
`affectedTasks`; absent scope is global/unknown. The scope survives unchanged in finding
history. Host-wide safety or authority failure must not be narrowed to unrelated tasks.
Only affected work waits; an actually owned repair lease can admit its exact repair
task without a static alias. Explicit pauses and failed-repair retry gates remain.
Known repair work cannot fall through as unrelated execution: every finding served
by that task needs a runnable lease and any required independent retry decision.
WAITING establishes ownership, not readiness. A fresh closure lifts an existing
affected hold only after owner application; unrelated work continues meanwhile.

Registry-backed services bind REVIEW to their own protected host. Current fixed-part POST/HEALTH checks every part and requires the complete union before a host verdict; a part alone grants nothing.
Legacy single-part `bindHost` requires a trusted owner, paused host, stopped model runs and no in-flight command; it preserves calls/runs/expiry without resetting budget or unpausing.
Legacy multipart rebinding is unsupported: retain history and diagnose an owned compatible transition, never downgrade or guess an unauthenticated configuration.

Initial adoption includes owned recovery or evidenced NO_CHANGE for existing maps,
module boundaries, graph/idea retention, tracker and artifact custody. Missing full
mapping is not completed mapping; a modularization proposal is not a sealed module.
Reuse existing Maintenance and preserve the remaining whole-project obligations.
Measure independently closed debt and retained product progress together; deferral,
renaming and deletion of a record are not repair. Follow `framework-activation.md`
for safe ordering and human decisions, without a compulsory whole-project rewrite.

Report product acceptance, control disposition, adoption limits, remaining owners,
actual continuation and total observed effort separately. Neither a green checklist
nor this document proves universal reliability or lower subscription consumption.
