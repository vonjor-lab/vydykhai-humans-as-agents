## Outcome And Non-Goals

Accumulate accepted reusable capability and retained DOD progress, with less
avoidable rework, human recall prompting and total attributable effort. A smaller
prompt, cheaper model, more commits or clean repository is not sufficient success.
The intended savings come from avoiding rework, rediscovery, duplicated modules,
unresolved debt and excess implementation context on subsequent tasks. Necessary
preparation/control may cost more initially; judge its retained downstream benefit,
not its isolated price or number of model calls.
No forced whole-project refactor, new mandatory microservices, permanent team of
managers, arbitrary branch cap, automatic self-modification or claimed invulnerability.

The user sees one outcome owner. Existing roles implement four responsibilities:
orchestration, factual preparation, execution and independent review. Discovery,
Lab and Maintenance are modes of scoped work, not extra obligatory departments.
Guard owns liveness/delivery; independently due Health Review owns project and
control-system assessment. Learning is a bounded reuse of completed evidence.

## Two Responsibility Circuits

The delivery circuit owns useful accepted DOD progress: orchestrator -> preparation
or bounded Discovery -> executor/Lab/Maintenance -> technical review -> product
acceptance and actual knowledge integration. Speed and cost are measured on
retained accepted outcomes, never on completion at any cost or manufactured PASS.

The independent control circuit owns honest admission and system health. Fresh
REVIEW examines readiness and completed acceptance; existing host observation
owns due triggers, authoritative findings, verdict delivery and response readback.
The host is the control center, not another conversational director. It separates
policy, observations and capabilities from the manager's account of its work.
It cannot suppress human authority, grant new spending, or autonomously replace
the manager. A rotation proposal retains its independent memory-migration gate.

Fresh means no inherited persuasive producer conversation or writable authority,
not no task knowledge. Supply the original request, accepted goal, public map,
source coverage, applicable policy and unresolved findings. The reviewer independently
chooses relevant sources. Durable findings survive; the reasoning context does not.
Use cheap deterministic checks for observable facts and bounded model judgment for
meaning/omissions. Reuse current valid proof rather than wake a model every turn.

These are responsibility boundaries, not two permanent agents or a new scheduler.
An unavailable checker leaves affected admission unconfirmed, preserves work and
raises one owned recovery need. The same host cannot certify its own external
availability: unsupported supervision is LIMITED, not an infinite watcher chain.

Output checks cannot undo completed side effects. Preparation checks cannot prove
all human history exists. A checker on an incomplete source index can confidently
miss the same obligation as the preparer. Therefore inspect coverage/provenance
and index-to-primary-source correspondence as well as packet completeness.
Unobserved tools outside the protected route remain outside its guarantee.
Guardrail libraries are optional adapters, not proof of these properties.

## Preparation Packet

A closed module is an accepted versioned capability with a public contract:
purpose and limits, typed inputs/outputs, errors/deficits, side effects and state
ownership, required permissions, dependencies, compatibility and reproducible
consumer examples. Its implementation and maintainer rationale are separate from
the concise consumer documentation. No hidden operator, model correction, fixture
patch or private side-channel may be necessary to obtain the promised result.
Algorithmic quality may have declared limits; automation cannot conceal failures.

Classify work as consume, change or create. Consumption uses a fixed artifact and
public interface without loading producer code. A composite owns its connections,
error handling and combined outcome; it does not bypass children's interfaces.
Changing public behavior requires an explicit contract decision, affected-consumer
proof and versioned rollout. Changing internals still requires scoped authority,
retained contract tests and resealing; a module is not immutable forever.
If public use fails, preserve the input/version/evidence and route a bounded
investigation to the owner, rather than turning every consumer into a maintainer.

One current packet binds task/outcome, scope, accepted baseline, module releases,
memory/source revisions, policy version, owners and model/effort choice. It carries:

- goal ancestry, intended result and explicit non-goals;
- accepted successes, exact entry conditions, preserved behavior and limitations;
- applicable past decisions/reasons, rejected paths, ideas and due commitments;
- consume/change/create boundaries, public inputs/outputs and affected consumers;
- permitted code/data/actions, dependencies, access and unresolved questions;
- plan, retained/new-behavior proof, documentation/memory effects and cleanup.

Every item has actual meaning and sources, not only ids. The independent reviewer
checks selection against the catalog and primary module/goal routes. The preparer
does not set its own sufficient coverage standard. An inaccessible source becomes
a named gap; safe research can recover it without recursively requiring a complete
implementation packet. Missing critical knowledge blocks only dependent mutation.
Readiness must have authorized current input/specification evidence and distinguish
the retained module baseline from the not-yet-executed consumer. Actual new output
belongs to subsequent verification and acceptance, not a circular admission test.

For action-changing decisions, preserve the short source chain from the original
claim through applicable corrections to current meaning. Distinguish primary
authority, reports of authority and inference; a newer date alone cannot establish
supersession. Check each citation against its actual supporting content. Unknown
baseline correspondence must stay unknown, not become either fabricated readiness
or a demand to prove a fact absent from the authorized evidence. Review findings
must identify an actionable semantic difference, not require a particular word.

Repeated tasks still check preparedness. Current matching evidence is reusable;
changed intent, scope, sources, baseline, policy or public contract invalidates
only affected proof. The executor receives the usable packet, not search history.
No compulsory cheap-model invocation if the current package already suffices.

### Graph, Map And Retrieval Acceptance

The graph retains meaning, corrections, ideas, rejected approaches, success
conditions, pivots and open return obligations. The map locates modules, public
contracts, versions, consumers and owned implementation. Consumer documentation
answers how to connect; maintainer documentation explains algorithms and rationale.
Link these existing sources; do not introduce a second graph or per-role registry.

Preparation starts from the ordinary request and the declared shared index, not a
maintainer-supplied list of leaf files or memory ids. Resolve aliases and goal/module
routes, intersect open commitments before relevance ranking, inspect affected public
consumers, and return applicable meaning with evidence. A composite boundary stops
recursive implementation loading, not inherited public obligations. Consumption
does not read producer source, including for preparation and control; a proven
public failure creates a separately authorized investigation boundary.
The implemented [memory-recall entry](../workflows/memory-recall.md) is a read-only
candidate locator in this route. Its limited source review and local tests do not
establish semantic completeness, consumer-safe prose or task admission.

Independent readiness must be able to inspect the same declared index and bounded
primary sources without accepting the preparer's selection as complete. Test an
omitted obligation and a sufficient unchanged packet. Structural validation, hash
matching, storage roundtrip and acknowledged readback are not semantic retrieval
or actual application. Inaccessible sources and stale/contradictory routes remain
named coverage gaps. No claim of complete memory beyond the evidenced source scope.

Option A qualifies two sequential ordinary tasks through the existing cycle. The
second must apply retained meaning and consume the same public capability without
leaf-source hints, producer internals, duplicate implementation or manual rescue.
An unchanged valid proof may be reused only with explicit dependency/validity
conditions. Relevant intent/contract/source changes invalidate it; unrelated work
must not force full rediscovery. All 87 criteria remain accounted for, with no
default PASS/N/A. General differential admission and an installed retrieval service
are not implemented. One narrower SDK route can independently admit a completed
effect on one approved dirty regular file outside the protected input set: exact
command identity, source/authority bindings and privately observed effects must
match a fresh health-domain approval. It is not a general source-change waiver,
external-writer lock, model assertion of confinement or permission to renew a
spent command. Relevant/unknown effects still refuse or remain in reconciliation.

## Closed Work Cycle

| Boundary | Preconditions and evidence | Outcome / owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | Actual request and current DOD; distinguish correction, idea and priority change | One owned increment or retained idea/decision; orchestrator |
| Locate and prepare | Public module/map/graph routes; current prior work and conditions | Bounded packet or named retrieval/Discovery question; existing owner |
| Admit | Every applicable catalog item disposed; independent selection/meaning review; actual current permission | Permitted action, scoped repair or explicit limitation; never self-approved omission |
| Execute | Worker current readback; exact permitted boundary; existing success preserved | First observable action and durable progress; executor |
| Reconcile delta | Material new source, changed goal/boundary, recall miss or repeated failure | Refresh affected packet/proof, reuse unchanged evidence and worker; no routine full restart |
| Verify | Exact Candidate/runtime, original successes and promised new behavior | Technical test/review evidence or owned correction; not the post-accept control verdict |
| Accept and retain | Orchestrator consumes the Return, performs accept-work against the goal, and completes or explicitly records pending memory/map/tracker and cleanup obligations | Durable product-acceptance record and actual retention/disposition evidence; orchestrator remains accountable for knowledge integration |
| Post-accept control | Actual orchestrator acceptance triggers fresh independent REVIEW of its completed cycle; consider all C01-C28 and inspect current evidence for applicable items | Source-bound verdict on acceptance, retention, debt and custody; no substitute pre-accept review or self-closure |
| Finish and continue | Post-accept verdict consumed with an observable disposition; blocking findings resolved before dependent actions, remaining debt owned | Accepted product result and control closure distinguished; next authorized action or concrete wait |

Implementation begins only after the supported admission route. Read-only
Discovery has its own bounded question/authority, not fictional implementation
readiness. A valid unchanged turn reuses admission and does not wake a second judge.
Repair stays within its known authority and cap; material new direction re-enters
the relevant boundary. No blind model escalation ladder or unbounded retry.

Task acceptance does not certify the whole parent or platform. Product completion
can coexist with an explicitly owned cleanup exception; the system-health view
must still show that exception. Required product docs and critical shared meaning
cannot be hidden as optional aftercare while dependent work starts uninformed.

The post-accept episode examines what the orchestrator actually accepted and
recorded, not a promise that it will later update memory or clean artifacts. Its
trigger and pending verdict survive outside the manager conversation. Missing
delivery or review remains unconfirmed control closure, never an implicit PASS.
The reviewer only publishes a verdict; the orchestrator owns corrective assignment
and an observed response. A fresh bounded readback checks repair against the same
criterion without inheriting an explanatory dialogue. Preserve the original
accepted artifact and finding history; do not silently rewrite acceptance or rerun
the product task. Hold affected dependencies, not unrelated authorized work.
This post-accept control does not move technical, security or permission checks
past irreversible actions. All 28 groups are considered for coverage, but current
applicable evidence is reused; this is not a fresh whole-project audit per task.
The owning publisher must expose the actually observed completed phase to the next
task. A pre-review PENDING promise cannot remain its current status after completion;
preserve that historical record separately, without rewriting decision meaning.

Accepted-cycle closure has three distinct outcomes: observed authorized CONTINUE,
owned WAIT, or completed DONE. WAIT has a durable owner/trigger/return/checkpoint;
DONE binds the accepted scope, DOD evidence and producer Return. Trusted SDK enrolls
the existing owner's typed record; fresh complete-catalog REVIEW independently
judges its source meaning before the owner consumes and applies the verdict.
The host observes bytes and records historical episode closure, not a fabricated
product command or continuing-current readiness. Selected current readback exposes
later drift or wait expiry as due attention; raw durable snapshots keep their
journal identity. Readback is not an atomic external-writer fence.
WAIT/DONE grant no execution, cannot erase blockers, UNKNOWN, pending review or pause,
and cannot stand in for renewed action admission. Expired wait needs fresh review.
Actual owner application and independently reviewed current-scope qualification
remain necessary; local structural verdict tests alone do not establish native
semantic adoption.

## Independent Health Cycles

Initial host diagnosis requires independent review and owner application. Later due
observation does not itself revoke unrelated execution. A blocking HEALTH finding
may bind explicit source-backed `affectedTasks`; missing scope remains global/unknown,
and existing scope cannot silently change. Admission reuses the actual owned repair
lease for its exact repair task, preserving human pauses and independent retry gates.
Every finding served by known repair work must be retry-ready; WAITING is ownership,
not execution readiness. New closure lifts an existing affected hold only after
owner application, without globally blocking previously unaffected work.

### Existing-Project Recovery

Every initial adoption of the new operating contract includes a recovery decision,
not just kit installation. Use the existing activation/adoption requirement and
Maintenance owner: verified NO_CHANGE for current healthy areas, otherwise an owned
recovery plan and proof of continuation. `doctor` remains installed-kit integrity;
its green output must never mean that a project has been healed. Do not add a
second manager, permanent doctor agent or competing State/graph.

The initial survey covers the whole declared project through seven views of the
existing records. They group, rather than replace, the C01-C28 responsibilities:

| View | Required recovery result | Catalog |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goals and plan | Current DOD hierarchy, priorities, agreement conflicts, explicit active/deferred ideas and return conditions | C01, C02, C03, C09, C11 |
| Successes and modules | Locate exact historical artifacts, entry conditions and assistance; distinguish reproduced public capability from aspiration; own packaging/repair | C04, C06, C07, C19 |
| Memory and ideas | Preserve source history, reconcile duplicate/superseded assertions, retain commitments and prove ordinary retrieval/application | C15, C16, C17 |
| Code map and docs | Whole-scope inventory, actual versus proposed boundaries, public docs, maintainer rationale and consumer routes | C13, C14 |
| Repository custody | Reconcile branches, worktrees, PRs, local-only/uncommitted work, runtimes and retained success versions | C10, C12, C26 |
| Shared work | Actual tracker/board, dependencies, result consumption, participant and unfinished-worker adoption | C18, C20, C23 |
| Control and security | Context/observer health, routing/cost, repeat failures, independent review and protected access/provenance | C05, C08, C21, C22, C24, C25, C27, C28 |

Do not load all code/history into an expensive context for the survey. Start with
actual inventories, public contracts and source ledgers; use bounded cheap retrieval
and independent coverage review. Unknown areas need an owner, not a healthy label.
Derive the target branch count from legitimate active work and retained custody,
not a universal small number. Squash/cherry-pick history and dirty work need actual
content/evidence reconciliation; age or Git ancestry alone never authorizes deletion.

Recovery sequence:

1. Preserve accepted revisions, data, unique work, exact prior outcomes and conditions,
   pending Returns, decisions and restore instructions. A screenshot is not proof
   of autonomous behavior. Keep assisted historical results as evidence, not releases.
2. Compare actual state to desired state and retain each finding with its original
   criterion, owner, evidence, repair proposal, dependency, attempts and next review.
   Show the person what already works, what blocks DOD, what will be preserved,
   what will change first and which decisions need agreement.
3. Name the safe boundary and finite review time. Read-only preparation can run
   during independent work. If the boundary slips, replan visibly instead of waiting
   forever for a demo. Checkpoint arrival is not permission to deploy/delete/refactor.
4. Execute bounded approved repairs in dependency order. Retain working modules;
   consolidate knowledge/custody before speculative rewrites. Branch deletion,
   memory cutover and public-boundary changes retain their actual approval gates.
   Failed repair preserves its count and original problem across restarts.
5. Independently prove retained successes and the selected new public-use path,
   graph retrieval, shared application and custody. Invasive repairs need a tested
   rollback; a backup name alone is insufficient. Whole-map completion and modularity
   remain separate facts. Restoring one route does not certify the whole platform.
6. Resume the nearest permitted DOD increment with current worker readback and
   observed progress, deliver the human report, retain nonblocking debt with owners
   and dates, and enroll normal Health Review. A plan to resume is not continuation.

Survey all accumulated issues; do not mandate fixing all of them before any useful
work can resume. Dependent work waits for critical repairs. Independent work keeps
its existing authority and pauses. Deferred work cannot be silently dropped, but
may be explicitly reprioritized or declined by the human with its consequences.
Recovery does not promise the absence of all future failure: it must detect a
repeat, retain the original evidence and prevent an unbounded equivalent retry.

`scripts/project-recovery.mjs` is an experimental read-only assessment over exports
of existing records, composing `assessCapabilityReadiness`. It is not wired into
the installed CLI/manifest and creates no tasks or mutations. Its seven-view input
is a test/prototype projection, not a new authoritative ledger. Receipt coverage
is not authenticated semantic acceptance; host readback/admission still needs proof.
The recovery binding includes the owner, safe-boundary observation and prior
findings. Historical failure and deadline changes cannot silently disappear.
Supplied trend and recovery views must agree on their obligations. Continuation
has its own binding to the recovery, next task, worker and authority; readback,
observed progress and the human report belong to that continuation. These bindings
detect stale pairing, not fabricated evidence; authenticity remains a host duty.

Project health and orchestration health share a cheap supported observation route
but have separate findings. The former concerns architecture/artifacts/progress;
the latter concerns preparation, authority, routing, truthfulness and continuation.
Their due records cannot be stored only inside the active manager's conversation.

An external host event/timer evaluates independent metadata and due obligations
even with no completed task or idle manager. It does not interpret algorithms.
One new meaningful anomaly or due bounded Health Review admits an existing fresh
REVIEW under its budget. Unchanged state reuses the disposition. Significant review
uses the accepted weekly/reset window; urgent scoped violations are not postponed
until an unrelated demo ends. Unsupported scheduling/telemetry is LIMITED.

Each finding keeps source criterion, affected boundary, original evidence, severity,
owner, deadline, attempts and resolution condition. The loop is:
observation -> independent finding -> owner response -> bounded repair -> current
proof -> independent disposition -> retained remaining debt -> productive continuation.
The manager cannot erase the finding by changing names, restarting or self-certifying.
If repair fails again without new evidence, preserve history and surface a concrete
decision; do not keep buying equivalent reviews. An actual pause prevents mutation,
not retention of pending work. A deadline is not permission and not a start delay.

## Independence And Cost

### Debt Reduction And Product Balance

Registering debt is not progress. At accepted review windows compare the inherited
cohort, newly discovered old problems, debt created by current work, independently
closed obligations and retained product outcomes. Preserve ids, criteria, origins,
attempts and evidence. Deferral/accepted risk is not repair; deleting a record,
renaming it or lowering its severity cannot improve the trend. Changed risk
classification needs explicit reconciliation outside a like-for-like comparison.

The target is sustained reduction of accumulated impediments alongside useful
product delivery, not a monotonically falling raw count after every observation.
A better survey can discover hidden debt. Critical/overdue obligations are reviewed
regardless of aggregate improvement. Stagnation across the agreed window threshold
requires an independent priority review and concrete recovery/replanning decision;
neither a new task name nor an explanation erases it. Review cannot grant mutation
against an actual human pause. Deferral remains visible with a finite return point.

Use existing Health Review and maintenance capacity, not another timer or agent.
A healthy window requires no manufactured refactor. Maintenance progress without
product progress triggers balance review rather than an automatic victory claim.
Count only outcomes with evidence in the same project scope and observation
window; historical or foreign successes cannot conceal current stagnation.
`scripts/debt-progress.mjs` is an experimental read-only comparator over trusted
evidence exports, not an installed trend collector or independent semantic judge.

Mechanical validation covers shape, bindings, changed versions, due conditions,
deduplication and receipt pairing. Semantic review covers relevance, omitted
meaning, realistic tests and architecture. Neither substitutes for the other.
Independent actors/permissions must be real host capabilities; different names in
JSON and content hashes do not authenticate separation. Native tools outside the
controlled route remain bypasses until observed/intercepted by the host.

Start with one supported adapter. Unsupported hosts retain advisory behavior and
explicit limits, not false mandatory enforcement. Reuse the existing scheduler,
outbox and roles. No recursive controllers watching controllers; where observation
of controller failure shares a failure domain, record that limitation and fallback.

Measure preparation, execution, semantic review, observation, recovery, retention
and coordination together. Preserve model/effort and cached/uncached/output usage;
allowance attribution may remain unknown. Added control must earn its overhead
through retained outcomes, fewer missed obligations and less rework. No automatic
quarter-hour inference or whole-graph loading is part of this specification.

## Learning And Human Interface

Retain small authorized evidence episodes on material outcomes/incidents, including
successful alternatives and confounders. Project review exports sanitized lessons
to framework maintenance; it does not install new rules directly. Human-collaboration
analysis is separate and must not assume interventions caused pre-existing failure.
Use the draft guidance in the design inventory. Users may offer ideas, correct
workers and change direction; they are not the scheduler or memory-retrieval engine.

Both learning streams reuse authorized outcome/incident evidence, not a paid judge
on every event. Retain the method/framework/model versions, intended outcome and
scope, actual sequence and costs (including failures and human minutes), knowledge
reused or missed, rework/duplicates, resulting debt, and unknowns/confounders. For
human collaboration distinguish recipient, timing and correction/idea/scope-change
from problems that already existed before intervention. Correlation is not cause;
guidance needs comparable cases, not blame or a personal score.

No installed collector or causal analysis is established by this specification.
Cross-project export requires explicit consent, minimization and source review;
exclude raw dialogue, private identities, secrets and customer data. A sanitized
lesson is a proposal to the maintainer, not authority to modify the framework.
Automatic recursive self-upgrade is outside 2.0 release scope: changes still need
goal-fit review, independent qualification, human release approval and safe adoption.

## Internal Consistency Decisions

- Independent control is separate judgment, not a second source of product priorities.
- Every task is prepared; every turn is not rediscovered. Current proof can be reused.
- Full applicable meaning matters, not an arbitrary small context or fixed graph cap.
- Closed modules hide internals from consumers, not public obligations or limitations.
- Lab success is not packaged autonomy; different data is not automatically a new contract.
- Rationale and ideas remain distinct from implemented/tested behavior.
- Health consideration has its own deadline; risky execution uses authorized safe boundaries.
- Review rejection is scoped; unrelated work can continue. Genuine unknown outcomes cannot retry.
- A merged PR is not a released/installed capability, and installation is not adoption.
- A dirty or rejected branch is not disposable; actual artifact custody precedes deletion.
- A healthy product result is not proof that the orchestration mechanism is healthy.
- Rotation and file-size thresholds initiate evidence-based decisions, not automatic churn.
