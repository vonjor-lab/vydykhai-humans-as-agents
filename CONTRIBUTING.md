# Contributing

Feedback, bug reports, and proposals are welcome through GitHub issues. Open an issue before preparing a substantial change so the direction and ownership terms are clear.

Substantive external contributions will not be merged until the contributor and Alexander Rozhnov execute a separate contributor agreement. That agreement must preserve the maintainer's ability to reproduce, modify, sublicense, relicense, and commercially license the contribution while the contributor retains any copyright they hold.

Until that agreement process is published, use issues for suggestions rather than submitting implementation or documentation PRs. Issue discussions are not confidential and do not transfer ownership of either party's pre-existing material.

Contributors must have the right to submit all material they provide and must disclose material third-party or AI-generated content whose terms or authorship may affect reuse.
